# UrbanMart Retail Analytics
## Project Overview

UrbanMart Retail Analytics is a retail sales and profitability analysis project developed using Microsoft Excel. 
The project analyzes sales data to understand revenue, profit, customer behavior, product performance,
and city-level performance, and uses these insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Problem
UrbanMart wanted to understand how its products, customers, 
and locations were performing and identify opportunities to increase profitability without significantly increasing the marketing budget.
The analysis focused on identifying high-performing products, understanding customer purchasing patterns, comparing city performance, 
examining revenue and profitability trends, and highlighting areas that require business attention.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 15 retail sales transactions covering different products, categories, cities, and customer types.
It includes information such as order date, product, category, city, customer type, quantity, unit price, and unit cost.

## Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed using Excel:
- Product revenue and profitability analysis
- Category performance analysis
- Customer type analysis
- City-level sales and profitability analysis
- Monthly revenue analysis
- Customer type and product purchasing analysis
- Revenue and profit per unit analysis
- Order size analysis
- Revenue concentration analysis
- Profit concentration analysis
- Revenue versus profit comparison

 ## Key Findings

Phone and Laptop generated KSh 635,000 in combined revenue, representing approximately 50.78% of total revenue.
Phone and Laptop generated KSh 161,000 in combined profit, representing approximately 51.85% of total profit.
Returning customers generated higher revenue and profit than new customers and had a higher average order value.
Nairobi recorded the highest overall revenue, while Kisumu had the highest average order value.
June was the strongest month for both revenue and profit, while February recorded the lowest revenue.
Blender had the highest profit margin, while Fridge had the lowest profit margin.
High revenue does not necessarily mean the highest profit margin, highlighting the importance of evaluating both revenue and profitability.

- Revenue by City
- Revenue by Customer Type

## Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:
1. Maintain adequate inventory of high-performing products, particularly Phone and Laptop, to protect revenue.
2. Reduce dependence on Phone and Laptop by improving the performance of other products.
3. Use cross-selling, bundles, and strategic promotions to increase items per order and overall profit.
4. Negotiate better supplier prices and reduce unnecessary operating costs to improve profit margins.
5. Improve customer service and retention while communicating product quality to encourage repeat purchases.
